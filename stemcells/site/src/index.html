01 · The arc

Thrown until it lands.

Every ask is a throw. It flies toward its goal and lands, or it falls short and asks you one clear question. Your answer is the next throw.

  1. FlyingIn the air. Your hands are free.
  2. InputIt fell short. One question, then it flies again.
  3. LandedIt stuck the landing. Read it when you like.
  4. SettledPut away. Off your mind.

02 · Shown, not told

Agents write walls. Stemcells answers in pictures.

It teaches every agent to answer the way you read, and draws it.

  1. A chartfor numbers.
  2. A diagramfor a flow.
  3. A previewfor a choice.
  4. One linefor where it stands.

03 · Like a stem cell

It becomes what you need.

A Cell starts plain and grows with the work. The app does the same: one chat or twelve, tabs or panes, your Mac or your phone, any agent you already use.

The shortest way from a thought to the thing

Bring your own agents.

Stemcells runs the coding agents you already use, on your own accounts and your own machine: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode and more. Your phone reaches your Mac over your own network. No telemetry. Open source.
